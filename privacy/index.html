Privacy

We use Microsoft Azure Application Insights to understand readership of the homepage and research articles. We collect page titles, page URLs without query strings or fragments, and referring site origins without their paths or query strings. Application Insights may derive approximate location from the request IP address; IP masking is enabled.

We do not use analytics cookies, local or session storage, persistent visitor identifiers, session recordings, or automatic click and network-request tracking. We do not send analytics when your browser enables Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control. Browser privacy tools and ad blockers may also prevent collection.

Telemetry is stored in Azure in East US with 90-day retention. Azure receives network information, including your IP address, when receiving telemetry. See the Microsoft Privacy Statement for more about Microsoft’s processing of personal data.
